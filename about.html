About the Chemistry Portal

Free AI-powered chemistry learning for Rwandan secondary schools

What this is

This portal supports chemistry teaching and learning for Senior 4, 5, and 6 students across all science combinations (MCB, PCB, PCM, ANP), aligned with Rwanda's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) and REB standards. It offers notes, quizzes, practice exercises, and full tests for students, plus AI-assisted lesson planning, exam generation, and scheme-of-work tools for teachers.

Who built it

Built and maintained by Jackson Habimana, a chemistry teacher at Association Carrefour Umurezi (E.S. Mudasomwa), Rwanda. The portal grew out of day-to-day classroom needs — generating REB-aligned lesson plans and exams faster, and giving students a self-study resource that matches what they're taught in class.

A note on AI-generated content

Lesson plans, exams, and summary notes on this portal are generated with AI assistance and formatted to match official REB structures. Teachers using these materials are encouraged to review content for accuracy before using it in the classroom.
